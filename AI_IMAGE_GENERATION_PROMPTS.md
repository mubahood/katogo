## PART 1 — Project Introduction (paste before any prompt if the tool supports context)

> You are generating advertising and branding images for **LugaFlix**, a movie streaming app from Kampala, Uganda. LugaFlix lets people watch movies and series translated to Luganda (the local language) on Android, iPhone, and computer. The audience is everyday Ugandans — young adults, families, boda riders, students — watching on smartphones.
>
> **Brand identity:**
> - Primary accent: vibrant orange (#FF7B00)
> - Background: deep dark navy / charcoal (#0D0D1A to #1a1a2e)
> - Secondary: deep red (#B71C1C) may be used sparingly
> - Text: white or off-white, bold modern sans-serif (like Montserrat/Poppins)
> - Mood: warm, energetic, proudly Ugandan, premium but accessible
> - Leave clear space for the LugaFlix logo and a "Download the App" button (we add these later)
>
> **STRICT RULES — every image must follow these (copyright & ad-policy safety):**
> 1. NEVER show real movie posters, film titles, movie scenes, or recognizable characters
> 2. NEVER show real celebrities, actors, or any named person (no "VJ Junior" etc.)
> 3. NEVER show logos of Netflix, YouTube, Marvel, Disney, or any existing brand
> 4. NEVER write the words "free", "Hollywood", "Netflix", or any movie title in the image
> 5. On phone/TV screens shown in the image, display only ABSTRACT content: a generic grid of colorful blank thumbnails, a play button, or soft glowing gradients — never real film imagery
> 6. People must be fictional, generated African/Ugandan characters — joyful, natural, everyday people
> 7. Any visible text must be only: "LugaFlix", "Watch in Luganda", or "Movies in Your Language"
> 8. Photorealistic or clean modern illustration style; no watermarks, no fake app-store badges

## PART 3 — Quick checklist before uploading any generated image to Google Ads

- No real movie poster, still, character, or title visible anywhere (zoom in and check screens)
- No real person's likeness (regenerate if a face resembles a known actor)
- No third-party logos, badges, or UI copied from Netflix/YouTube/Play Store
- No "FREE", no VJ names, no film names in any baked-in text
- Correct pixel dimensions before upload (resize/crop, don't stretch)
- Keep the source file — Google may ask you to verify you own the asset
